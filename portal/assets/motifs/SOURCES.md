# Sourced SVG ornaments

## Bronze drum
- Work: Trống đồng Đông Sơn.svg
- Artist: Tom Mai (Baokhang48812002)
- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Trống_đồng_Đông_Sơn.svg
- License: CC0 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Original: `dong-son-commons.svg`.
- Web variant: `dong-son-gold.svg`. Removed white background circle and editor metadata, recolored to gold, rounded path coordinates to three decimal places. Artwork geometry retained.

## Flying crane
- Work: Asian Cranes, uploaded by j4p4n; based on public-domain illustrations by Kono Bairei (1844–1895).
- Source: https://openclipart.org/detail/322149/asian-cranes
- License: Public domain / CC0; https://openclipart.org/share
- Original: `asian-cranes-source.svg`.
- Web variant: `crane-flight.svg`. Cropped SVG viewBox to the upper flying bird, removed editor metadata, rounded coordinates to three decimal places. Kept original ink/ivory colors.
- Used as decorative bird artwork, not a claim that this Japanese illustration is a Vietnamese historical motif.

Earlier handmade bird/drum/woven SVGs are no longer used by the live interface. Grain remains a small procedural texture.

## Fine-outline flying bird (current interface)
- Source: Stork silhouette, https://openclipart.org/detail/303907/stork-silhouette
- License: Public domain / CC0 (Openclipart).
- Original: `flying-bird-outline-source.svg`.
- Web variant: `bird-fine-outline.svg`; geometry retained, fills removed, fine gold outline added, editor metadata removed. The body and wings are transparent.
- Replaces the filled Asian Cranes artwork in the live interface. Five birds make a timed pass once when a chapter enters the viewport; there is no scrub or persistent bird position tied to scrolling.

## Current split between flight and decoration
- Five-bird timed fly-by restored to the original filled `crane-flight.svg` (Asian Cranes / Openclipart, cited above).
- Fixed page ornament now uses `crane-decor-outline.svg`: a transparent fine gold contour derived from the same crane silhouette with SVG morphology. It has no body fill.
- The top-view stork outline and heraldic candidate are not referenced by the interface.
